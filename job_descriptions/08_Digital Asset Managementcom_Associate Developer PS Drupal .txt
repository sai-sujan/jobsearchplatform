About Acquia

Acquia empowers the world’s most ambitious brands to create digital customer experiences that matter. With open source Drupal at its core, the Acquia Digital Experience Platform (DXP) enables marketers, developers, and IT operations teams at thousands of global organizations to rapidly compose and deploy digital products and services that engage customers, enhance conversions, and help businesses stand out. Headquartered in the U.S., Acquia is a Great Place to Work-CertifiedTM company in India, is listed as one of the world’s top software companies by The Software Report, and is positioned as a market leader by the analyst community. We are Acquia. We are building for the future and we want you to be a part of it!

About The Team

You will be joining a high-energy delivery team dedicated to building world-class digital experiences for Acquia’s global clients. As an Associate Developer, you aren’t just a "coder"—you are a foundational part of a pod that includes Senior Technical Architects and Project Managers. You’ll have the unique opportunity to be mentored by some of the most influential contributors in the Drupal community while working at the intersection of Open Source and Artificial Intelligence.

Our team is at the forefront of the AI-integrated web, leveraging the Drupal AI module ecosystem and Acquia’s AI Assist to build smarter, more intuitive applications. You will help implement cutting-edge features across all industries. We value curiosity and a "growth mindset" as we explore how AI can accelerate our development and enhance the experience for our customers.

About The Role

Your mission is to turn architectural designs into functional, high-performing code while embracing the next generation of intelligent web development. As an Associate Developer, you will focus on the hands-on development of Drupal sites, leveraging a modern stack that follows industry standards, Acquia best practices, and the emerging Drupal AI ecosystem.

This role is designed for someone who has mastered the fundamentals and is ready to professionalize their workflow within the Acquia ecosystem. You will not only build traditional web components but also implement AI-augmented features—such as semantic search, automated content categorization, and dynamic personalization—that define the future of Digital Experience Platforms (DXP).
