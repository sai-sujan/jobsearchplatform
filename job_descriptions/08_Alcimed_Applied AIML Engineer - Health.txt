About the job

Are you passionate about data and have an entrepreneurial spirit?




Since 1993, Alcimed has been exploring and developing uncharted territories, growing to over 200 people across 7 offices in Europe, the USA and Asia. At our core, we help our industry clients innovate and develop new markets in the Lifesciences sectors, with a very impact-oriented and grounded approach.




Building on this foundation, in 2024, Alcimed successfully launched Nautilus.ai, its Digital & AI activity dedicated to the design & development of function-specific, bespoke, ethical, and secure digital & AI tools. 




At Nautilus.ai, we help Lifesciences industry players leverage data; harness and ethically apply state-of-the-art digital and AI technologies to build solutions which serve, and foster human added value - thereby enhancing their positive impact on healthcare systems and patients.




Your missions

As an AI/ML Engineer, you will work at the crossroads of healthcare innovation, artificial intelligence, and consulting. Embedded in lean, agile project teams, you will co-develop enterprise-grade AI-powered digital solutions directly within our clients’ ecosystems.




Your main responsibilities include:

Translate healthcare and pharma business needs (R&D, medical affairs, commercial, etc.) into robust AI and ML solution designs with the most appropriate approach depending on client constraints and requirements: security, cost, performance, reliability and explainability
Build and optimize data pipelines, fine-tuning strategies, and evaluation frameworks for domain-specific use cases
Design, develop, and deploy AI-powered features leveraging LLMs, agentic AI systems, and modern ML techniques 
Collaborate closely with software engineers to integrate AI components into fully featured applications
Act as a technical referent on AI topics within project teams and contribute to internal best practices
Maintain direct interaction with clients throughout the project lifecycle, in an agile, product-oriented consulting approach




The real plus of your mission? You are in direct contact with our customers to help them move forward and shape the world of tomorrow.




Your profile

Master’s degree or PhD in Computer Science, Data Science, AI, Biomedical Engineering, or a related field
1–3 years of hands-on experience building AI / ML systems, ideally in healthcare, life sciences, or regulated industries
Strong proficiency in Python and modern ML / AI tooling
Practical experience with LLMs, including at least one of:
RAG architectures
Fine-tuning techniques
Model evaluation and benchmarking
Agentic AI systems or MCP servers, using modern frameworks (e.g. LangChain, LlamaIndex, AutoGen, CrewAI or similar)
Familiarity with open-source LLM ecosystems is a strong plus
Ability to work in lean teams, take ownership, and make technical decisions
Strong interest in healthcare, pharma, and applying AI to real-world impact
Comfortable in client-facing environments, able to explain complex AI concepts to non-technical stakeholders
Curious, entrepreneurial mindset, with a strong appetite for innovation and teamwork




Key skills:

 LLMs, Agentic AI, RAG, Fine-tuning, Python, Applied AI, Healthcare AI, Life Sciences, LangChain, LlamaIndex, MLOps, Consulting, Product Development
