Position Description

We are seeking a skilled Jr Full Stack Developer to design, build, and deploy scalable web applications. The ideal candidate has strong hands-on experience with Python, React.js, PostgreSQL (AWS RDS), as well as familiarity with NoSQL databases and core AWS services like S3, EC2 and Lambda. Experience working in Agile environments with JIRA, strong communication skills, and the ability to collaborate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders are essential.
