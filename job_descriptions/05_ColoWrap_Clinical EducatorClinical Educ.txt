About the job

Company Description
 

ColoWrap is an innovative medical technology company specializing in the development of external compression solutions aimed at improving colonoscopy and other endoscopic procedures. As a pioneer in its field, ColoWrap is dedicated to advancing technologies that enhance patient outcomes and simplify medical practices. The company is committed to providing groundbreaking tools that improve the safety and efficacy of procedures worldwide.


 Role Description
 

This is a full-time, on-site position located in Lawrenceburg, IN. As a Clinical Educator, you will play a key role in delivering education and training regarding ColoWrap’s innovative medical technologies. This includes providing in-depth product and procedural training to healthcare professionals, conducting on-site and off-site workshops, and engaging in clinical research initiatives. You will also contribute to the development of educational materials, collaborate with internal teams, and ensure the consistent application of best practices.


 Qualifications
 
Proficient in Clinical Education, with the ability to develop and deliver effective training programs
Knowledge of Critical Care Medicine and Medicine to support clinical best practices and address technical inquiries
Experience in Training healthcare professionals and staff on medical technology and procedures
Background in Research to support evidence-based educational materials and clinical initiatives
Strong organizational, communication, and presentation skills
Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams and adapt to a fast-paced workplace
A valid nursing, medical, or related clinical certification or degree; advanced certifications are a plus
