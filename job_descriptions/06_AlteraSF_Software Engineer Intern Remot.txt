About the job

About Altera Strategy Foundry Inc. (AlteraSF)

AlteraSF is an AI-first startup reimagining how companies evaluate and hire talent. Our mission is simple but radical: to replace résumé guesswork with verified truth and measurable fit. We build intelligent screening and analytics tools that help organizations detect résumé exaggeration, identify true capability, and dramatically reduce recruiter workload, all without adding complexity to existing hiring workflows.




At AlteraSF, we’re not just building another recruiting tool. We’re redefining hiring integrity. Our platform helps employers find who can actually do the work, not just claim it on paper.




Role Overview

We’re looking for a highly motivated Software Engineer Intern to work directly with our Technical Co-Founder on building and scaling core product functionality. This is a long-term internship running through August 2026, designed for students who want sustained ownership, deep technical growth, and meaningful exposure to real production systems.




This is not a passive or observational internship. You will write production code, participate in technical decisions, and see your work go live.




Location / Duration / Eligibility

Location: Remote
Duration: Through August 2026
Eligibility: Currently enrolled college students (Undergrad or Master’s)




Responsibilities

Work closely with the Technical Co-Founder on feature development and system design
Design, build, and ship features across the product stack
Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code
Participate in code reviews and technical discussions
Debug issues and improve performance, reliability, and scalability
Contribute to internal tools, APIs, and data pipelines as needed




What We’re Looking For

Currently enrolled in a US college or university (Computer Science, Engineering, or related field)
Strong fundamentals in software engineering and data structures
Experience with one or more programming languages (Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Java, or similar)
Familiarity with modern web technologies, APIs, or cloud infrastructure is a plus




Compensation

This is an equity-only internship. There is no cash compensation associated with this role. There is no guarantee of future employment, though strong performers may be considered for extended or additional roles at the company.
