📢 Why Are We Hiring AI Engineers?

We are growing fast — and we’re looking for AI Engineers to join our team. With rising demand from clients and internal projects, we need innovators who can design, build, and optimize AI-driven systems that keep businesses running smarter.

This isn’t just about coding. It’s about building scalable workflows, creating AI-powered solutions, and applying automation to solve real-world problems across industries. If you thrive on making systems efficient, intelligent, and impactful, we want to hear from you. 🚀

Responsibilities Of The AI Engineer

Your responsibilities include:


⚙️ Workflow Automation & Integration
Design, implement, and optimize automations using tools like n8n, Make.com, and Zapier
Build reliable integrations across Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack/Teams, WhatsApp, CRMs, and more
Ensure automations are error-resilient, scalable, and proactively monitored with smart error handling and notifications
🤖 AI-Powered Development
Develop AI-driven workflows such as transcription pipelines, chatbots, meeting assistants, and voice-to-form solutions
Leverage APIs like OpenAI, Claude, ElevenLabs, and Read AI to deliver intelligent user experiences
Fine-tune, prompt, and optimize large language models (LLMs) for task-specific use cases (e.g., summarization, classification, Q&A)
Design and deploy AI assistants, recommendation systems, and intelligent search tools that improve user experience
📊 Data & Reporting Systems
Create and maintain data dashboards and automated reporting pipelines using AirTable, Google Data Studio, HubSpot, and Sheets/Excel
Ensure data accuracy and visibility across teams and projects
🛠 DevOps & Monitoring
Deploy and manage AI models in production environments with continuous monitoring and performance tuning
Establish A/B testing and evaluation frameworks for AI-driven features
Ensure cost optimization when running models and automations on cloud platforms
🤝 Collaboration & Documentation
Partner with stakeholders to understand needs, map workflows, and translate requirements into technical solutions
Document workflows, integrations, and automations to ensure transparency, scalability, and easy maintenance
