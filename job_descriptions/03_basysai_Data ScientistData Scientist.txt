About the job

Company Description

 

basys.ai leverages agentic AI to streamline processes like prior authorization and utilization management for healthcare providers and health plans. Our mission is to reduce administrative burdens and enhance financial performance, enabling providers to focus on delivering quality patient care. Our efforts have gained recognition in industry-leading platforms such as TechCrunch and Forbes. Based in Boston, MA, we are committed to revolutionizing healthcare operations with cutting-edge technology and innovation.




 Role Description

 

This is a full-time, on-site role located in Boston, MA for a Data Scientist at basys.ai. The Data Scientist will work on analyzing complex healthcare data to develop insights and solutions that drive operational efficiency and performance improvements. Key responsibilities include building predictive models, deploying machine learning algorithms, creating data visualizations, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to solve unique challenges in healthcare administration.




 Qualifications

 

Strong skills in Data Analysis, Machine Learning, and Statistical Modeling
Proficiency in programming languages like Python, R, or equivalent for data manipulation and algorithm development
Experience with Data Visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, or Matplotlib
Familiarity with Big Data frameworks like Hadoop, Spark, or equivalent
Understanding of healthcare data standards, prior authorization processes, and utilization management is a plus
Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to analyze and interpret complex datasets
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, or a related quantitative field
Strong communication skills to effectively collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders
