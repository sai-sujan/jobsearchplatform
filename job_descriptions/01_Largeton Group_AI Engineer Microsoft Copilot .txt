About the job

AI Engineer – Microsoft Copilot Studio - Remote


 Build and configure GenAI solutions using Microsoft Copilot Studio (prompts, topics, flows, connectors)
 Design and test prompt logic and AI interaction patterns
 Integrate Copilot Studio with Azure OpenAI and external APIs
 Support RAG-style workflows, connecting AI to structured/unstructured data sources
 Develop and maintain Power Automate flows for AI-driven processes
 Write simple Python or PowerShell scripts for automation, testing, and data preparation
 Collaborate with senior engineers to enhance AI workflows and integrations
 Participate in testing, debugging, documentation, and process improvements
 Join technical and client sessions as required
 Deliver hands-on engineering support for GenAI and Copilot initiatives
 Help improve reusable AI components, prompts, and accelerators
 Contribute to internal standards for AI quality, performance, and usability
 Expand AI engineering skills through real-world, production-focused projects


Required Experience/Skills


 2+ years in software development, automation, or platform engineering
 Hands-on experience with Microsoft Copilot Studio (required)
 Experience building/supporting GenAI applications or AI-driven workflows
 Strong with Power Platform, especially Power Automate
 Working knowledge of Azure services (Azure OpenAI, Functions, Logic Apps, App Services, Storage)
 REST API integration experience
 Basic coding/scripting ability (Python or PowerShell)
 Understanding of data flows, authentication, and cloud security basics
 Strong problem-solving skills and technical curiosity


Nice To Have


 Power Platform certifications (PL-100, PL-200, PL-400)
 Experience with Dataverse
 Exposure to RAG patterns, embeddings, vector databases
 Familiarity with Git, CI/CD, DevOps practices
 Experience with proofs of concept or internal AI tools
