About the job

About The Company

Prime Therapeutics (Prime) is a innovative pharmacy benefit management (PBM) company committed to transforming healthcare through data-driven solutions and personalized care. Unlike traditional PBMs, Prime operates with a purpose beyond profits, focusing on improving health outcomes and connecting care for those we serve. Our mission is to build a future where pharmacy benefits are more accessible, affordable, and effective. We leverage advanced technology, industry expertise, and a dedicated team to deliver exceptional service to our clients and members. Prime’s culture emphasizes integrity, collaboration, innovation, and diversity, fostering an environment where employees can thrive and make a meaningful impact in healthcare.

About The Role

The Data & Reporting Analyst at Prime Therapeutics plays a critical role in supporting strategic decision-making and operational efficiency through comprehensive data analysis and reporting. This remote position involves working closely with various business units to develop, maintain, and enhance reporting solutions that provide meaningful insights into pharmacy and healthcare data. The analyst is responsible for transforming complex data sets into clear, actionable reports and dashboards, facilitating data-driven decisions across the organization. The role requires a detail-oriented professional with strong technical skills, problem-solving abilities, and the capacity to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams. The successful candidate will contribute to continuous process improvements, support system enhancements, and ensure data accuracy and integrity.

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and relevant work experience
High school diploma or GED required
Minimum of 2 years of experience in data analysis and reporting
At least 1 year of SQL experience
Eligibility to work in the United States without sponsorship

Responsibilities

Develop and deliver reporting solutions adhering to internal standards and best practices by collecting, validating, interpreting, and organizing data into meaningful reports for designated client groups
Maintain and enhance the reliability, efficiency, and functionality of existing reports and dashboards
Facilitate team request intake sessions to gather reporting requirements and prioritize projects accordingly
Analyze data issues and recommend quality and system improvements by collaborating with system owners and maintaining data iteration processes
Train users and team members on reporting tools and processes as needed
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to improve upstream data processes and system integrations
Support ad hoc reporting and data analysis requests to meet organizational needs
Participate in continuous process improvement initiatives to optimize reporting workflows
Perform other duties as assigned to support business objectives
