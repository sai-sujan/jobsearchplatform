About The Company

Endurance IT Services is a leading provider of innovative technology solutions dedicated to empowering businesses through cutting-edge IT services. With a strong focus on customer satisfaction and operational excellence, we deliver comprehensive solutions that include cloud computing, cybersecurity, software development, and IT consulting. Our team of experienced professionals is committed to helping organizations optimize their technology infrastructure, enhance productivity, and achieve their strategic goals. At Endurance IT Services, we prioritize integrity, innovation, and collaboration to foster long-term partnerships with our clients across various industries.

About The Role

We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated IT Professional to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will be responsible for managing and supporting our clients' IT infrastructure, ensuring seamless operation and security of their systems. The ideal candidate will possess a strong technical background, excellent problem-solving skills, and the ability to communicate complex technical information effectively. This position offers an exciting opportunity to work on diverse projects, collaborate with a talented team, and contribute to the growth and success of our organization and clients.

Qualifications


Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
Proven experience in IT support, network administration, or systems management.
Strong knowledge of operating systems such as Windows, Linux, and macOS.
Experience with cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
Familiarity with cybersecurity best practices and tools.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
Ability to troubleshoot and resolve technical issues efficiently.
Certifications such as CompTIA, Cisco CCNA, Microsoft Certified Solutions Expert (MCSE), or equivalent are preferred.


Responsibilities


Provide technical support and troubleshooting assistance to clients across various IT systems and infrastructure.
Manage and monitor network performance, security, and reliability.
Implement and maintain cloud solutions, ensuring optimal performance and security.
Assist in the planning, deployment, and upgrade of hardware and software components.
Develop and document IT policies, procedures, and best practices.
Conduct regular system audits and vulnerability assessments to identify and mitigate risks.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver tailored IT solutions that meet client needs.
Stay updated with the latest industry trends and emerging technologies to recommend improvements and innovations.
