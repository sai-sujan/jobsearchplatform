About the job

CodaMetrix is revolutionizing Revenue Cycle Management with its AI-powered autonomous coding solution, a multi-specialty AI-platform that translates clinical information into accurate sets of medical codes. CodaMetrix’s autonomous coding drives efficiency under fee-for-service and value-based care models and supports improved patient care. We are passionate about getting physicians and healthcare providers away from the keyboard and back to clinical care.

Job Description:

At CodaMetrix, the machine learning (ML) and AI team is responsible for the invention, analysis, and deployment of new ML techniques using healthcare data to improve administrative and clinical medicine.

We are looking for an ML Engineer I to join our machine learning team and play a pivotal role in translating proof-of-concept ideas to scalable solutions. You will work with a multidisciplinary team of engineers, data scientists, academicians to gather requirements and understand performance criteria to deliver solutions that optimize operational efficiency, and drive innovation in the healthcare industry. The Machine Learning Engineer I reports to the Director of MLEN.

Key Responsibilities:


Establish processes for data and modeling lifecycle through managing the transitions from proof-of-concept to production. 
Collaborate with machine learning, engineering, and product development teams, for deployment of new machine learning techniques and follow deployments, tracking issues, and successes. 
Ability to quickly adapt to dynamic requirements in terms of programming, system design and solutioning
Work on data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model evaluation to ensure robust and accurate predictive models. 
Produce technical documentation, software, and supporting test code
Participate in all team activities, including agile ceremonies and reviews of other engineers designs and code


Qualifications:


Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related field
Proficiency in Python, data manipulation libraries (Pandas, Numpy), deep learning frameworks (MLFlow, PyTorch with focus on Large Language Models)
Understanding of natural language processing, relevant machine learning subfields
Foundational grasp of Distributed computing, scalable data transformation, and event-based processing using Spark, preferably using the Databricks platform
Knowledge of SQL and NoSQL Databases
Proficiency in working within a Linux ecosystem and experience with Docker
Effective verbal and written communication skills with both management and peers


Beneficial Experience:


Experience working in a startup / fast paced environment
Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Databricks) for model deployment and scaling
Statistical background and/or exposure to ML techniques and programming
Familiarity with healthcare regulations and privacy requirements (e.g., HIPAA). 
Experience working with healthcare data and knowledge of US healthcare systems
Previous experience with data visualization tools and reporting.
