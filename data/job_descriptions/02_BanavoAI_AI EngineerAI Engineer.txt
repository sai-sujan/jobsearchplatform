About the job

AI Engineer (Banavo)




What you’ll do




	•	Take high-level goals and ambiguous requirements, break them into a clear plan, drive execution end-to-end with minimal oversight, and proactively communicate progress, risks, and tradeoffs (written and verbal) across engineering, product, and stakeholders.

	•	Use AI (ChatGPT/Co-Pilot/Perplexity/AI-native IDEs/Google, etc.) extensively and naturally to research, design, develop, and test solutions to meet business and product goals.

	•	Build and ship AI-powered features: agentic workflows, RAG, tool-calling assistants, deterministic AI-to-SQL, and reliability guardrails/evals.

	•	Develop and maintain Python/FastAPI services (including async/streaming endpoints where needed).

	•	Build and operate retrieval/data layers for AI (chunking, embeddings, indexing, reranking, caching) across structured + unstructured sources.

	•	Collaborate with product and data teams to deliver user-facing AI workflows and internal platform capabilities.

	•	Improve operability: logging, metrics, alerting, runbooks, and incident response hygiene.




Tech stack you’ll work with




	•	AI/LLM: LLM APIs, tool/function calling, RAG/embeddings, prompt/versioning, evals/guardrails

	•	Backend: Python, FastAPI

	•	Data/Retrieval: S3, Athena/Trino, vector/graph retrieval, caching patterns

	•	Cloud/Platform: AWS, EKS, Docker, ECR

	•	GitOps: FluxCD (HelmRelease, Kustomize)

	•	IaC: Terraform

	•	Observability: structured logging, metrics/tracing fundamentals




Required qualifications (tools/frameworks)




	•	Python with FastAPI for building and operating production AI services (async + streaming where needed).

	•	LLM application engineering: agentic workflows, tool calling, RAG, and grounding against business data.

	•	Multi-agent orchestration best practices: tool interfaces/contracts, planning vs execution separation, error handling, retries/timeouts, state management, and deterministic output formats.

	•	Evals/reliability: measuring quality, reducing hallucinations, and optimizing latency/cost with versioned prompts.

	•	Docker: building and shipping images; working with registries and runtime configuration.

	•	Production ownership: ability to debug across app + data layers using logs/metrics and improve reliability over time.

	•	Experience building multi-tenant SaaS patterns (tenant isolation, RBAC, per-tenant config, auditability) and security best practices (secrets hygiene, safe data access boundaries).




Preferred qualifications




	•	AWS and EKS experience shipping and operating production services.

	•	Kubernetes fundamentals: Deployments/Services/Ingress, requests/limits, probes, HPA/PDB, and hands-on debugging with kubectl.

	•	FluxCD GitOps workflows: sources, reconciliation, dependencies, HelmRelease/Kustomizations, drift management.

	•	Terraform: modules, environments/workspaces, remote state, and building/operating cloud infrastructure.

	•	AI-to-SQL and analytics agent experience (safe, deterministic query generation; schema grounding).
