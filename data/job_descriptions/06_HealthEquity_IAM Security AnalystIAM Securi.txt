About the job

Our Mission

Overview

 We are  CONNECTING HEALTH AND WEALTH.  Come be part of remarkable. 

HealthEquity is growing our Identity and Access Management Security Team and looking for a Security Analyst who is passionate about protecting people, data, and access. If you want to do meaningful work that supports millions of members while collaborating with a team that lives our values every day, this role is for you.

As a Security Analyst on our IAM and Governance team, you will play a key role in secure and efficient identity lifecycle management across HealthEquity technologies. You will support employees and business partners by ensuring they have the right access at the right time while maintaining a strong security mindset and delivering exceptional customer service. This position is ideal for someone who enjoys problem solving, partnering across IT and the business, and contributing to a collaborative, learning‑focused team.

What You’ll Be Doing

 Access Governance: 


 Implement access governance processes to support compliance, security, and IAM best practices. 
 Identity Lifecycle Management: Manage complete identity lifecycles including provisioning, de‑provisioning, and account maintenance. 
 Drive automation to streamline workflows and improve operational efficiency. 


Risk Management


 Partner closely with cybersecurity teams to respond to incidents and support corrective actions. 
 Balance a security‑first mindset with high‑quality customer support. 


Team Cooperation


 Collaborate with IAM teammates to support initiatives and foster continuous learning. Coordinate with cross‑functional partners to ensure successful delivery of IAM tasks. 


What You Will Need To Be Successful


 Experience with IAM tools such as SailPoint IdentityNow, NERM, or OneTrust 
 Working knowledge of on‑prem Active Directory and Entra ID 
 Strong Excel skills 
 PowerShell scripting experience 
 Ability to influence others and navigate difficult conversations to protect privacy, strengthen security, and meet audit requirements 
 Experience partnering with end users to develop, document, and implement business roles and procedures 
 Strong analytical skills to identify issues, understand risks, gather data, draw conclusions, and drive resolution 
 Ability to implement process improvements and educate others when improvements roll out 
 Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience 


This is an hourly/non-exempt position: At this time we are unable to hire hourly roles within the following areas: Alaska, California, Hawaii, Nevada, New Jersey, New York, Oregon & Rhode Island.

This is a remote position.
