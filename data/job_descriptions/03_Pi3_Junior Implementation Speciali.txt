About the job

Pi3AI is a fast-growing partner in the fintech / SaaS ecosystem helping companies automate and optimize their financial operations through the Simetrik platform.

We are looking for an Entry-Level Implementation Analyst to join our team in the US and support financial automation projects for clients across the region.

If you’re passionate about data, finance, problem-solving, and learning fast-growing technology platforms — this role is for you.




What You’ll Do

Support the implementation of financial reconciliation and automation projects for clients.
Work directly with operations, finance, and technical teams to understand client workflows.
Configure and document the use case, data logic, and reconciliation rules.
Troubleshoot issues and support the stabilization phase of each implementation.
Prepare and update functional documentation for each client project.
Collaborate closely with senior specialists to ensure timely and high-quality delivery.




Who We’re Looking For

Recent graduate or early-career professional in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Industrial Engineering, Business Analytics, or related field.
Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
Excel / Google Sheets: Advanced (mandatory).
Interest or exposure to SQL, Power BI, or other data tools (nice to have).
Excellent communication and problem-solving abilities.
Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, client-facing environment.
Located in the United States.




What We Offer

100% remote (US-based).
Opportunity to specialize in a high-demand fintech platform.
Fast career growth: Implementations → Senior → Project Management → Product.
Direct exposure to global fintech clients.




This role is perfect for you if…

You’re early in your career and want to grow FAST in fintech.
You love solving operational and financial problems using data.
You want hands-on experience with real client implementations.
You can work in EST time
