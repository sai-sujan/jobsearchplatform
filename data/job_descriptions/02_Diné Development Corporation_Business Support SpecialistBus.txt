About the job

DDC is seeking a Business Support Specialist to support the Professional Services Vertical's business development team by providing administrative, coordination, and research support. This role executes and manages key business development processes, maintains accurate and timely data, manages documentation and tracking systems, coordinates internal workflows, and ensures business development information and communications are clear, organized, and accessible to support effective execution.

Job Duties And Responsibilities

The Business Support Specialist supports the Professional Services Vertical's business development team by completing a variety of administrative and coordination tasks. Responsibilities include assisting with basic research on potential clients and opportunities, maintaining lead and contact information in tracking systems, and helping prepare and format documents, proposals, and presentations. The role also involves scheduling virtual meetings, tracking follow-up actions, organizing digital files, and supporting email communications. Additional duties may be assigned as needed to support team goals and business development initiatives.

Core Skills & Competencies Include


 Organization and follow-through
 Professional communication
 Analytical and research skills
 Collaboration and teamwork
 Confidentiality and discretion
 Initiative and problem-solving


Job Requirements (Education/Skills/Experience)

This position requires a high school diploma or equivalent and proficiency with computers and online productivity tools (ex. Microsoft Office, SharePoint, Salesforce). Candidates must possess strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to communicate clearly and professionally in both written and verbal formats. The role requires the ability to follow instructions, manage time effectively, meet deadlines, and work independently in a remote environment. A reliable internet connection is required.

About Us

Diné Development Corporation (DDC) is a Navajo Nation-owned family of companies that delivers IT, professional, and environmental solutions to advance the missions of federal, state, and tribal government agencies. As thought leaders and innovators, our team of specialists builds client-centric solutions that solve critical challenges faced by defense, civilian, and healthcare organizations.
