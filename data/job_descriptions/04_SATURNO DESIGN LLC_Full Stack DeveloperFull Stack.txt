About the job

Saturno Design is hiring a full stack developer to help us with a variety of web-based projects, including mid to large size websites and implementation of our industry-leading automated proposal software.




Work with an award-winning, Collaborative Team. We have an integrated team that combines design and development to provide clients with one point of contact for all their software needs. We are a close-knit team working with a friendly, collaborative culture with a flattened organizational structure. Our approach enables team members to have independence and leeway to make their own decisions and provide important input during the development process, without any corporate-style bureaucracy and politics.




This position is remote for those in Massachusetts, Oregon or Colorado. FTE opportunity.




Here are some of the things you’ll be doing in the first few months:

Work closely with project managers to build and help maintain client websites and implement and customize our proprietary software products.
Interpret business requirements and to convert them into workable solutions.
Collaborate with other team members in project planning, code reviews, and testing.
Daily stand up meetings to set priorities for the day.
Learn new skills as you work with our experienced team of developers.




What skills are needed?

The position requires the following experience:

7+ years of full-stack web development
High comfort level with ASP.NET Web Forms (Legacy Products), ASP.NET MVC (Greenfield Products), JavaScript, jQuery, C#/VB
Working knowledge of MS SQL Relational Databases
Experience with .NET Core (.NET 8+), Dependency Injection, Tag Helpers, View Components
Familiarity with Microsoft dev tool ecosystem: Visual Studio 2026, TFS+git
Solid communication skills for technical topics and business requirements/logic analysis




High regard for experience with:

Public facing web site best practices (SEO, ADA/WCAG)
Microsoft Azure Hosting (PaaS oriented)
Advanced SQL
Integrations/Data Processing
ASPOSE document generation components
Agile/Scrum workflow
