About The Role

We are seeking a motivated Business Intelligence Software Developer to join our team. In this role, you will support the development of data models, reports, and automation solutions that drive insights across the organization. Your responsibilities will include building and maintaining Power BI reports and dashboards, developing data models, and integrating various data sources such as SharePoint Online and SQL databases. This position offers an excellent opportunity for someone with foundational experience in Power BI, SQL, and Python to grow into a broader analytics engineering and Power Platform skillset. You will work closely with senior team members to design scalable BI solutions, optimize data structures, and support key reporting initiatives, ensuring data accuracy and adherence to governance standards.

Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Information Management, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or equivalent experience
Experience in BI development, data analytics, or related technical roles
Hands-on experience with Power BI, including DAX and Power Query
Understanding of data modeling concepts such as star schema, relationships, and measures
Practical experience with SharePoint Online lists, including creation and calculated columns
Proficiency with SQL and familiarity with SQL Server
Basic to intermediate Python scripting skills
Strong troubleshooting skills for data issues and problem-solving processes
Excellent communication skills and ability to collaborate with technical and non-technical stakeholders
Exposure to Power Platform tools such as Power Automate or Power Apps is a plus
Familiarity with version control tools like Git
Knowledge of BI governance, workspace management, or deployment pipelines
Ability to handle multiple tasks and adapt to changing priorities
Curious, motivated, and eager to learn new tools and technologies
Attention to detail with a focus on producing accurate and reliable outputs

Responsibilities

Build and maintain Power BI reports, dashboards, and datasets to support business needs
Develop DAX measures, calculated columns, and optimized data models within Power BI
Create and enhance Power Query (M) transformations and ETL logic to ensure data quality and consistency
Assist in building and maintaining Power BI semantic models for efficient data analysis
Create, configure, and maintain SharePoint Online lists, including calculated columns and data structures
Integrate SharePoint Online lists and other data sources into Power BI for comprehensive reporting
Write and maintain SQL queries, stored procedures, and functions to support reporting requirements
Provide basic SQL Server administration and troubleshooting support as needed
Utilize Python scripting for data manipulation, automation, or analytics tasks
Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand reporting requirements and translate them into technical solutions
Ensure data accuracy, documentation, and compliance with governance standards
Participate in continuous improvement of BI solutions and workflows
