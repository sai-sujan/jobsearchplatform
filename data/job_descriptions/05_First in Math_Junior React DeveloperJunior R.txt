About the job

Company Description

First in Math® Online Program is a leading educational platform dedicated to empowering students with lifelong mathematics skills. Through engaging and effective tools, our program has become a renowned resource in classrooms across the globe. Having reached over one million students in more than ten countries, we are committed to fostering a new generation of innovative thinkers. Our mission is to provide learners with valuable skills in a fun and impactful way.




We’re a small, family-owned company building engaging, educational web platforms that make a real difference for users. Our team is collaborative, hands-on, and supportive — we care deeply about clean code, thoughtful design, and helping each other grow.




Role Description




What You'll Do

Work closely with senior developers to build and enhance front-end features using React.
Contribute to clean, efficient, and maintainable code.
Help design and implement responsive, accessible, and engaging user interfaces.
Participate in code reviews and learn modern development best practices.
Gain real-world experience in a collaborative environment where your contributions matter.




What We're Looking For

A recent graduate or early-career React developer with a strong proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript and ES6+ features.
Hands-on experience with React is required
Familiarity with and can utilize Front-End Technologies such as HTML, CSS and modern front-end build tools.
Experience with state management libraries such as Redux
Comfortable collaborating locally with our team during shared hours.
A strong communicator who enjoys problem-solving and continuous learning.
Experience with version control systems such as Git.

 

Why You’ll Love Working With Us

Direct mentorship from experienced developers
Opportunity to see your work have a real, visible impact.
Friendly, flexible, and supportive team culture.
Real opportunity to grow your skills in a modern front-end environment.




Contract-to-Hire Role (Primary):




This position is structured as a contract-to-hire role, with the intent to convert to full-time employment after 3–6 months, based on performance and mutual fit.




We believe contract-to-hire creates the best outcomes for both sides — giving candidates the opportunity to demonstrate their abilities in a real-world setting while ensuring a strong long-term fit.

Supportive, collaborative team environment with real ownership
