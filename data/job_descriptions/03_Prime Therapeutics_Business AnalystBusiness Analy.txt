About the job

About The Company

Prime Therapeutics (Prime) is a distinguished pharmacy benefit management (PBM) organization committed to making a meaningful difference beyond mere profits. With a mission to connect care effectively for those they serve, Prime is dedicated to transforming the pharmacy landscape through innovative solutions and strategic initiatives. The company prides itself on fostering a purpose-driven environment that prioritizes patient care, operational excellence, and technological advancement. As a forward-thinking organization, Prime Therapeutics continually seeks talented professionals to join its team and contribute to shaping the future of pharmacy services. The company values diversity, integrity, and collaboration, creating an inclusive workplace where every employee can thrive and make an impact.

About The Role

The Business Analyst (BA) at Prime Therapeutics plays a pivotal role in executing small-scale projects and acting as a liaison among various stakeholders including business operations, technology teams, clients, vendors, and project management offices. The primary focus of this role is to understand, analyze, and facilitate business needs, requirements, and the impacts of strategic initiatives, operational shifts, compliance mandates, and technological or operational enhancements. The Business Analyst will independently manage small projects or collaborate within larger project teams to ensure seamless communication and delivery. Responsibilities include defining and validating business needs, conducting stakeholder analysis, assessing capability gaps, and determining suitable solution approaches. The role also involves planning analysis activities, conducting elicitation sessions, managing requirements traceability, and defining user acceptance testing strategies. The ideal candidate will adhere to Prime’s project lifecycle methodology and standards, ensuring quality and consistency in all deliverables. This position offers an excellent opportunity for professionals seeking to contribute to a purpose-driven organization while developing their skills in business analysis within a dynamic environment.
