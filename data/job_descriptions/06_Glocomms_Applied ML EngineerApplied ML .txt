About the job





Applied Machine Learning Engineer



We're seeking an Applied Machine Learning Engineer to design and deploy intelligent systems that streamline infrastructure workflows. This role involves working hands-on with advanced technologies-such as large language models (LLMs), knowledge graphs, and modern retrieval systems-to build scalable, agent-based solutions.

You'll prototype rapidly, experiment with emerging approaches, and take projects from concept through production. If you thrive in dynamic environments and are passionate about applying AI to real-world challenges, this position is for you.



What You'll Do


Design and implement large-scale machine learning systems that deliver measurable product value.
Build and maintain end-to-end evaluation pipelines using real-world data.
Collaborate cross-functionally to translate business needs into ML-driven solutions.
Prototype and iterate quickly, adapting to feedback and evolving requirements.


What We're Looking For


Experience: 5+ years in applied machine learning or a related role, ideally in a fast-paced or high-growth environment.
Technical Skills: 
Strong ML fundamentals: classification/regression, clustering, dimensionality reduction, evaluation, probabilistic modeling.
Expertise in at least one applied ML domain (e.g., NLP, search, statistical modeling).
Proficiency in Python and TypeScript; familiarity with common ML libraries and data engineering tools.
Mindset: Problem-solving focus, strong communication skills, and comfort with iterative development.
Bonus: 
Experience with vector/hybrid search.
Dataset curation or synthetic data generation.
Contributions to open-source ML tools.
Familiarity with Go or other systems languages.


Who You Are


A builder who thrives in collaborative, fast-paced environments.
Excited about working in-person at a modern office.
Passionate about startups and zero-to-one product development.
Enjoys working with ambitious, systems-thinking teammates.


What We Offer


Competitive compensation and equity.
Comprehensive health, dental, and vision coverage, plus retirement benefits.
Unlimited PTO and wellness perks.
Support for late-night work and commuting.
Tools and resources to help you do your best work.
