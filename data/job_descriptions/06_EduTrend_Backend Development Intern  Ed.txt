About the job

About Us

EduTrend is a remote-first edtech startup building a behavioral tracking and analytics platform for students with special needs. With development underway and a beta launch planned for Fall 2025, we’re seeking backend development interns who are ready to build reliable APIs, work with real data models, and ship production-quality features.




What We’re Building

EduTrend enables schools and specialists to collect, track, and analyze behavioral data for early intervention and support planning. Backend interns will focus on the systems that power data integrity, performance, and scalability across our web and mobile clients.




Intern Responsibilities

Build and maintain backend services using Node.js and TypeScript
Design and implement GraphQL schemas, queries, and mutations
Model, store, and query data using MongoDB
Implement authentication, authorization, and role-based access control
Use Redis or other caching strategies to improve performance
Collaborate with frontend developers to support end-to-end user flows
Write and maintain unit and integration tests
Contribute to CI/CD pipelines, debugging, and production readiness




Required Skills

Proficiency in JavaScript or TypeScript
Experience building backend services with Node.js
Experience designing and implementing GraphQL APIs
Experience working with MongoDB or other NoSQL databases
Familiarity with Redis or backend caching strategies
Experience writing backend tests (unit and/or integration)
Exposure to Docker and CI/CD workflows
Strong understanding of API design, data modeling, and backend architecture
Familiarity with Git and collaborative workflows
Clear communication and ability to work async in a remote team




What You’ll Gain

Experience working on a real product preparing for beta launch, with features used by educators in real classroom environments
An understanding of how backend systems support product requirements, permissions, and user roles in an applied setting
Exposure to collaborative software development in a remote-first startup, including planning, reviews, and async communication
Opportunities to take ownership of scoped backend tasks and see them through from design to release
Experience working alongside frontend, design, and product teams in a full-stack environment
Insight into how engineering decisions are made in an early-stage startup, including tradeoffs between speed, quality, and scalability
Constructive feedback and mentorship aimed at improving code quality, system thinking, and professional engineering practices
Portfolio-ready contributions that reflect real-world product development rather than academic exercises




Join Us

If you’re a back-end developer or development-focused student who cares deeply about building reliable, secure, and scalable systems that support real users, we want to hear from you! Due to being a start-up with a limited budget, this internship would be unpaid. In return, you’ll work on a real product, with real users, contributing to production back-end features, APIs, and data flows that are actively used in classrooms while producing meaningful portfolio work that reflects real-world industry practice and helps improve outcomes for students with special needs.
