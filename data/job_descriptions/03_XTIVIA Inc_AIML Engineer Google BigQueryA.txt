Description

XTIVIA - AI/ML Engineer (Google BigQuery)

Work Location: Remote

Duration: 3 Months (Initial contract/SOW, with potential for extension)

How you will make a difference

XTIVIA is seeking a Google BigQuery AI/ML Engineer to help design and build next-generation, AI-enabled analytics capabilities on top of Google BigQuery. This role will focus on developing natural-language query agents, forecasting solutions, and AI-powered insights that are securely embedded into enterprise collaboration tools such as Microsoft Teams.

Key Responsibilities


Design and implement natural language query agents that translate user questions into optimized BigQuery SQL.
Build and operationalize Gemini-powered AI workflows for analytics, summarization, and forecasting.
Develop time-series forecasting models using BigQuery ML and integrate results with Gemini for explainability.
Implement governance, access control, and auditability for AI-driven data access.
Integrate AI capabilities into Microsoft Teams for seamless user adoption.
Collaborate with data engineering, security, and business teams to ensure scalable and secure solutions.
Optimize query performance, cost controls, and data reliability in BigQuery.


Requirements

What you bring to your new team


Strong experience with Google BigQuery and SQL at enterprise scale.
Hands-on experience with BigQuery ML or similar forecasting/statistical modeling frameworks.
Experience working with LLMs (Gemini, Vertex AI, or equivalent) in enterprise settings.
Knowledge of natural language to SQL patterns and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
Experience integrating data or AI services into collaboration tools (Teams, Slack, or similar).
Strong understanding of data security, RBAC, and enterprise governance.


How you add even MORE value


Experience with Vertex AI and model orchestration.
Background in retail, supply chain, or enterprise analytics.
Familiarity with prompt engineering, AI observability, and usage analytics.
Experience designing internal productivity tools or analytics platforms.
